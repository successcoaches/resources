Courseware

Everything you need to add and enroll AP students on the Courseware (Edmentum) platform — video walkthroughs and the email templates that go with them.

← Back to Hub

AP courses are delivered through Courseware (Edmentum). Adding a student is a two-step process: first create the student in the Administration Center, then place them in their course in Courseware. Log in at auth.edmentum.com/elf/login using the Account Name EXHS. The two walkthroughs below show each step start to finish.

Email Templates
The messages that go with Courseware enrollment. Click Copy, then paste into your email and fill in the student-specific details.
Courses Are Ready
Sent once courses are assigned and ready to access
Subject: Your Excel High School Courses Are Ready! Hi, Good news, your courses are now active in your student account and you're ready to get started! Please read this email thoroughly, as it contains important information and resources to use throughout your enrollment. Your Current Courses You are enrolled in: {Courses} - The Orientation course is designed to help you navigate learning with Excel. - You may exchange a course for another in the same subject within 30 days of assignment. - If you would like to choose different electives, please review the Course Catalog (https://www.excelhighschool.com/course-catalog/?filter=true&subject=elective) and let me know your preferences. Logging In - AP Courses (Courseware, our AP platform): Courseware Login (https://auth.edmentum.com/elf/login) | Account Name: EXHS | Username: _____ | Password: _____ (Note: the Courseware login requires an Account Name. Be sure to enter EXHS in that field along with your username and password.) - Honors, Standard & Electives (LearnStage): Student Login (https://identity.learnstage.com/exceled/sch-23-000a/basic?redirect_url=https://live.learnstage.com/exceled/excelhighschool/login/auth) - Deadline to complete courses: (date) Getting Started - Set a consistent study schedule (example: 8am-3pm, Monday-Friday, with a lunch break). - Study in a quiet space at a desk or table. - Take handwritten notes, they improve understanding and can be used on quizzes and exams. Course Materials (Important) Each of your courses includes a section that lists the required and optional materials for that course. Review it carefully once you log in, you're responsible for acquiring these materials. Some are free online, while others require purchase. Resources - Success Coach Corner (https://successcoaches.github.io/corner/): Academic resources including tools for time management, pacing, and study skills. - Writing Lab (https://writinglab.exceled.com/): Worksheets designed to help you write your English essays. - If your essay did not receive a passing grade, please follow these instructions (https://drive.google.com/open?id=15vvnK4sGAVSmXd_8m5mgfghMFVxz5ga3) to review the feedback provided and identify areas for improvement. - Peterson's Test & Career Prep (https://link.gale.com/apps/PTCP?u=excelhigh): A variety of test prep, career, and scholarship resources. Getting Help - For AP courses, access the "Messages" tab on Courseware. There, you can write a message to any of your instructors for support. - For all other support, please submit a helpdesk ticket on your LearnStage account. AP Testing You're responsible for registering for AP exams locally, Excel does not administer them. Next window: May 2027. Use this link to find a location near you: Find a Testing Location (https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/help-center/ap-exam-i-want-take-isnt-offered-my-school-what-should-i-do) Graduation Planning - Review the Course Planning Guide (https://www.excelhighschool.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/EHS-Course-Planning-Guide.pdf) to understand credit options (21.5 vs. 24 credits). - Students planning to attend a 4-year university should notify their Success Coach to ensure they are placed on the 24-credit path. Please reach out with any questions. Happy studying! Best,
Platinum Welcome
Welcome message for Platinum students (AP note included where applicable)
Hi [Student Name], Welcome to the Excel High School community! Here's what we need to move forward with enrollment: 1. School Records – Please have your previous school send official transcripts to records@excelhighschool.com. International coursework must go through Scholaro: https://www.scholaro.com/apply/excel (Course-by-Course Report for high school, General Report for middle school). Official records cannot be accepted directly from students or families. Unofficial records are welcome in the meantime to help us start reviewing. 2. Success Coach Pro – A free program offering 6 one-on-one meetings with a Success Coach. Reply to this email if you would like to take advantage of it. 3. ElevatED – An add-on program ($1,000) for 11th/12th graders offering one-on-one coaching, college and career planning, and enrichment activities. A note on AP courses (if applicable): If you're taking AP courses, they're delivered through Courseware, our AP platform, and you'll receive full login details once your courses are ready. Platinum also includes AP access, so if you're interested in adding any AP courses to your schedule, just let me know. If you have any questions or need anything as you get started, you can reach out by replying to this email or by submitting a helpdesk ticket, and we'll be glad to help. Best, Excel High School
